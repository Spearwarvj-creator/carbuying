## Description

A car dealer asks what monthly payment you would be comfortable with. That question is the whole trick: stretch the loan and the payment falls while the total climbs. On the home page, 36 months is $989 a month and $38,601 in total; 84 months is $491 a month and $44,246, which is $5,645 more.

Deal Desk is a free car deal calculator that shows you that before you sign. No account, and your numbers stay on your device.

Try it: https://carbuying.v2stack.com

The one rule: the calculator makes every number. The AI only reads your deal, explains a word or helps you practise the conversation, and a guard throws away any answer that contains a figure that is not in your deal.

In this video (No. 6 of V2Stack, where I build and ship one real product every week):
- Why a lower payment can cost more
- A walk through the six-step calculator, with a hand check of the $603 a month
